Palm Monorail: status, stations and fares

Status: Palm Monorail is temporarily suspended. The operator states that the service is temporarily suspended until further notice for maintenance.

The Palm Monorail runs from Palm Gateway to Atlantis Aquaventure on Palm Jumeirah (4 stations). It is run by a private operator and needs its own ticket; Nol cards are not used.
