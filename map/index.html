A schematic map of the Dubai Metro Red Line (35 stations) and Green Line (20 stations), the Dubai Tram (11 stops) and the Palm Monorail (4 stations): 68 stations in all, with current names and every interchange.

How to read the map

  • Red Line: Centrepoint to Life Pharmacy, with a branch from National Paints to Expo 2020. Red Line stations
  • Green Line: e& to Creek. Green Line stations
  • Dubai Tram: Jumeirah Beach Residence 1 to Al Sufouh, one way round a loop at JBR. Tram stops
  • Palm Monorail: Palm Gateway to Atlantis Aquaventure, temporarily suspended. Status and fares
  • Interchanges in one station: BurJuman, Union.
  • Walking links between stations (dotted on the map): Dubai Marina and Sobha Realty (~6 min on foot); Jumeirah Lakes Towers and DMCC (~6 min on foot); Palm Jumeirah and Palm Gateway (~8 min on foot); Jumeirah Beach Residence 1 and Dubai Marina Mall (~12 min on foot).
