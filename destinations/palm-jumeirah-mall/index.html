Step by step: DXB Airport Terminal 3 to Palm Jumeirah Mall

Palm Monorail is temporarily suspended. This route uses it, so it cannot be travelled right now.

82–109 min · 2 changes · 19 min walking · AED 7.5 + AED 10 monorail

Red Line walk Dubai Tram walk Palm Monorail

  1. Go to Airport Terminal 3~5 min from DXB Airport Terminal 3, follow the Metro signs, the station is linked to the terminal
  2. Take the Red Line towards Life Pharmacy or Expo 2020Board at Airport Terminal 3 · 20 stops · ~47 min · Get off at Sobha Realty
  3. Walk to Dubai Marina~6 min on foot, signposted
  4. Take the Dubai Tram towards Al SufouhBoard at Dubai Marina · 4 stops · ~10 min · Get off at Palm Jumeirah
  5. Walk to Palm Gateway~8 min on foot, outside the stations
  6. Take the Palm Monorail towards Atlantis AquaventureBoard at Palm Gateway · 2 stops · ~6 min · Get off at Palm Jumeirah Mall
  7. Walk to Palm Jumeirah MallShort walk from the station; we don't have a reliable time for this one
