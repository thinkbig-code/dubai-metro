Dubai Frame by metro: nearest station and how to get there

The nearest station to Dubai Frame is Max on the Red Line. It is about 15 minutes on foot, through Zabeel Park. The station has also been called Al Jafiliya.

Step by step: DXB Airport Terminal 3 to Dubai Frame

38–42 min · no change · 20 min walking · AED 5

Red Line

  1. Go to Airport Terminal 3~5 min from DXB Airport Terminal 3, follow the Metro signs, the station is linked to the terminal
  2. Take the Red Line towards Life Pharmacy or Expo 2020Board at Airport Terminal 3 · 8 stops · ~18 min · Get off at Max
  3. Walk to Dubai Frame~15 min, through Zabeel Park
