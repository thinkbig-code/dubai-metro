Dubai Metro Red Line: stations, branches and hours

The Red Line runs from Centrepoint to Life Pharmacy, with a branch from National Paints to Expo 2020. Trains from Centrepoint run either to Life Pharmacy or to Expo 2020, so check the destination on the train: for The Gardens to Expo 2020 take a train to Expo 2020; for Ibn Battuta to Life Pharmacy take a train to Life Pharmacy; to go from one branch to the other, change at National Paints.
