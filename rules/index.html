Dubai Metro rules, fines and Nol fares

What a new rider needs to know: which Nol card to use, what a trip costs, what is fined and how much, and the mistakes people make in their first week. Fines are from the RTA; check rta.ae for any change.

Silver (standard)AED 3AED 5AED 7.5
Gold (Gold Class cabin)AED 6AED 10AED 15
Red ticket (paper)AED 4AED 6AED 8.5

Metro, tram and buses share 7 fare zones. A journey can last up to 180 minutes with up to 3 changes. The fare is taken when you tap out; you need at least AED 7.50 on a Silver card to enter. Children aged 5 and under travel free; students, seniors and Social Affairs beneficiaries get 50% off with a personal (Blue) card, and people of determination travel free.

How to pay

  • Nol card (Silver, Gold or personal Blue): tap in at the gate and tap out at the end.
  • Red ticket: a paper ticket for a few trips, sold at the ticket machines.
  • Digital Nol card on supported Samsung and Huawei phones. iPhone, Apple Pay and bank cards cannot be used at the gates yet: the RTA is rolling this out, with completion expected by early 2027.

Common mistakes

  • Sitting in Gold Class with a Silver card: AED 100, and inspectors check it.
  • Too little balance: you need AED 7.50 on a Silver card to enter, and the fare is taken when you leave.
  • Drinking or eating on the train or platform.
  • Using a family member's discounted Blue card: it counts as using someone else's card (AED 200).
  • Expecting to tap an iPhone or a bank card at the gate: not possible yet.
  • Heading to Terminal 2 by metro: only Terminals 1 and 3 have Red Line stations.
